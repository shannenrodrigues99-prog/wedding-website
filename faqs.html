Is there anything I should know about attending a Church Ceremony?
If this is your first time visiting a Catholic Church, welcome! Out of respect for the Church and the ceremony, we ask that guests keep noise to a minimum. During the ceremony, there will be times when guests are invited to stand, sit and kneel. Please feel free to follow along as you are comfortable.

During Communion, those who are Catholic are invited to receive the Eucharist. If you are not Catholic, you are welcome to remain seated or come forward with your arms crossed to receive a blessing.
