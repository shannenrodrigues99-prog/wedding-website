FAQs

01
How and when should I RSVP?
Please visit the RSVP tab at the top of the page and follow the link to submit your response. We kindly ask that you RSVP by 1 December 2026.
02
Can I bring a plus one?
Due to venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate the guests formally named on your invitation. Thank you for your understanding.
03
What is the dress code?
Please refer to the Schedule page for the dress code listed for the event you are attending.
04
Are dietary requirements catered for?
Absolutely. Please include any dietary requirements when submitting your RSVP, so we can make the proper arrangements.
05
Is parking available?
Parking is available onsite. We encourage all guests who will be drinking to plan ahead and arrange a designated driver, rideshare or taxi.
06
What time should I arrive?
Please refer to the Schedule page for arrival and event times. We recommend allowing a little extra time for parking and getting settled.
07
Can I take photos?
We would love for you to be present with us during the formal moments. You are welcome to take photos throughout the celebrations, but please be mindful of our photographer and videographer.
