Can I take photos during the ceremony?
You are welcome to take photos during the ceremony. We simply ask that you remain mindful of our professional photographer and videographer and avoid stepping into the aisle during the ceremony.
Is there anything I should know about attending a Church Ceremony?
If this is your first time visiting a Catholic Church, welcome! During the ceremony, there will be times when guests are invited to stand, sit and kneel. Please feel free to follow along as you are comfortable. We simply ask that everyone remain mindful of the sacred setting and keep noise to a minimum.

During Communion, those who are Catholic are invited to receive the Eucharist. If you are not Catholic, you are very welcome to remain seated or come forward with your arms crossed to receive a blessing.
Is there anything I should know about attending a Hindu Ceremony?
If you have never attended a Hindu ceremony before, please don’t worry. Everyone is warmly welcomed, and you do not need to be familiar with any of the customs or traditions beforehand.

Throughout the ceremony, you will see a variety of prayers, rituals and traditions taking place. Simply follow along, observe and participate as you feel comfortable. Our family and friends will also be nearby and happy to guide you if needed.

Most importantly, we hope you enjoy experiencing this meaningful part of our wedding celebrations with us.
Can you cater for dietary requirements?
Absolutely! Please include any dietary requirements when submitting your RSVP, and we will do our best to accommodate them.
Do you have a wedding registry?
